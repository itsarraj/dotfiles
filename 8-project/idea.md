The concept is a dynamic location safety platform designed to help people assess the true safety of a neighborhood/ place/city before they travel or move there. Currently, standard maps and official crime statistics fail to capture the nuanced, on-the-ground reality of a location—such as broken streetlights, pedestrian safety, or how secure an area actually feels at night.
To solve this, the platform will generate hyper-local safety insights by aggregating and scoring data from three core pillars:
Crowdsourced Public Reviews: Capturing the actual lived experiences of locals and visitors.
Local News Articles: Using Natural Language Processing (NLP) to extract and weigh recent safety-related events.
Official Statistics: Integrating verified government and police reports.
By fusing qualitative text with hard statistics, the platform bridges the gap between raw data and real life, empowering users with the context they need to make safe, informed decisions about where they live and go.
