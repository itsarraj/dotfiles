# ADHD Dopamine Detox & Anti-Sabotage Protocol

## 1. Why Rage Bait + 2x Speed + ADHD Is a Lethal Attraction

This is not a character flaw; it is neurochemistry.

- ADHD is a baseline dopamine deficit: The ADHD prefrontal cortex has lower baseline dopamine and norepinephrine. Boredom does not feel neutral; to an ADHD brain, low stimulation feels physically intolerable and agonizing.
- Rage bait triggers noradrenaline (the emergency drug): Anger, injustice, and conflict instantly flood the brain with adrenaline and noradrenaline. It gives your nervous system artificial energy and sharp alertness without requiring any effort from you. It feels like plugging an undercharged battery into a high-voltage outlet.
- 2x speed matches brain hyperactivity: Normal 1x video speed feels like torture because your processing speed leaves empty bandwidth, allowing intrusive thoughts or restlessness to creep in. 2x speed floods the sensory buffer completely so you do not have to sit with yourself.

When combined, rage bait at 2x speed is a digital speedball: continuous noradrenergic arousal and instant dopamine hits with zero cognitive effort.

## 2. Why You Are Sabotaging Your Goals Intentionally (Why Why)

Intentionally wasting time when you care deeply about your goal is called self-handicapping and threat avoidance. Here is what is happening under the surface:

- Protection from the real test: Your goals (Assembly, Rust, systems dominance, escaping the wage trap) are enormous and matter to you with deadly seriousness. If you give 100% effort and struggle or fail, the emotional verdict is terrifying: "What if I am not smart enough?" But if you sabotage yourself with rage bait, you give your ego a safe excuse: "I did not fail because of my intellect; I failed because I was distracted."
- The dopamine contrast shock: Going from a high-voltage rage-bait firehose to staring at a quiet, silent page of CS:APP or debugging gdb feels like jumping into an icy lake. The brain anticipates that painful withdrawal and screams to stay in the high-stimulation zone.
- Subconscious rebellion: You recently set very strict rules, purged your old interests, and tightened the screws on your life. When you squeeze yourself too hard without pacing, the subconscious rebels against its own warden. Wasting time becomes a perverted way to reclaim control: "I decide what I do, even if it destroys my day."

## 3. How to Detox (The Protocol)

Trying to fight this with pure willpower does not work on an ADHD brain. You need structural friction and contrast management.

### Rule 1: The 15-Minute Airlock (Never switch directly)
You cannot jump straight from 2x rage videos into reading low-level code. The contrast will defeat you every time.
- When you catch yourself bingeing, do not try to work immediately.
- Turn off the screen, put your phone in another room, and lie down on the floor or sit looking at a blank wall for 15 minutes. No audio, no screens, no reading.
- Let the dopamine receptors reset from the flood. Once baseline quiet returns, the mental friction of opening your editor drops by 80%.

### Rule 2: Hard Friction on Video Feeds
The algorithm knows your neurological weak spots better than you do. Remove the temptation from your immediate reach:
- On Linux, block feed domains via /etc/hosts or browser extensions like Unhook (which removes the YouTube home feed, comments, and recommendations entirely).
- If you need a technical video, never watch it in the browser. Download the specific URL via yt-dlp to your local drive and play it offline with mpv. This eliminates the algorithm loop.

### Rule 3: Lower the Activation Energy to 2 Minutes
When you think "I have to grind assembly for 6 hours," your brain panics and retreats to videos.
- Shrink the task until your brain stops resisting: "I will open the terminal, assemble one 10-line file, and run it in gdb. After that, I can stop if I want."
- Once the first action is taken, momentum takes over and ADHD hyperfocus can engage in the code.

### Rule 4: Replace Visual Rage with Physical Friction
When your brain craves high intensity and noradrenaline, give it to your body, not your eyes:
- Do 30 push-ups, hang from a pull-up bar until failure, or take a cold shower.
- Physical grit discharges the restless nervous energy without polluting your mind with internet garbage.

### Rule 5: Kill the Shame Spiral Immediately
The cycle is always:
Distraction -> Guilt and self-disgust -> Brain seeks escape from bad feeling -> More rage bait.
- The moment you realize you slipped, do not beat yourself up or declare the day ruined.
- Acknowledge it objectively: "My brain was dopamine-hungry, and I fell for a cheap trap. Resetting now."
- Take a deep breath, run the 15-minute airlock, and start the next block clean.
