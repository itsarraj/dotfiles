# What Not To Do (Quarantine & Anti-Pattern List)

A living filter compiled from past failures, energy leaks, and habits that derailed progress. If an action is on this list, it is an active hazard to low-level systems engineering and wealth goals.

## 1. Career & Technical Traps

- No Godot / game dev: Zero leverage for low-level systems engineering. Escape from Duckov and RPG games are dopamine escapes when systems work gets hard.
- No micro consumer apps / startup illusions: Freeze the Play Store app. Do not build unvalidated SaaS tools just because AI subscriptions exist. B2B infrastructure only when ready.
- No stack hopping: Do not jump between Python, Node.js, TypeScript, Go, and frontend frameworks. Stick strictly to the systems core: Assembly, C, C++, Rust, and Linux internals.
- No Web3 / crypto rabbit holes: Do not waste cognitive bandwidth on Solana courses, token setups, or blockchain SDKs. They do not build serious systems credentials.
- No planning addiction: Do not spend days redesigning roadmaps, schedules, and book lists. Learning happens by writing code and reading line by line, not by planning.

## 2. Information & Media Traps

- No pornography: Zero adult content, zero sexual stimulation on screens. Complete reset of dopamine baseline.
- No gaming: Zero PC games, zero mobile games, zero casual gaming apps. Pure dopamine leak that robs focus and hours.
- No music: Zero background music, playlists, or auditory pacifiers during deep work or rest. Train the mind to tolerate absolute silence and high cognitive friction.
- No movies or fiction series: Zero movies, entertainment streaming, Netflix, or web series.
- Only curated offline documentaries: Screen intake is restricted exclusively to a pre-selected playlist of educational and scientific documentaries. Watch intentionally to extract insight, never for passive entertainment.
- Only approved reading queue: Strictly read from the designated, capped reading queue (maximum 1 technical, 1 mindset). Zero buying, downloading, or skimming unvetted books on impulse.
- No online rabbit holes: No YouTube home feeds, no Shorts, no Instagram reels. If a video is educational, download it offline via yt-dlp.
- No information hoarding: If a book, course, or tool cannot be applied in the next 18 hours or current milestone, do not stockpile it.
- No tab stockpiling: Close tabs at the end of every work session. Never hoard hundreds of open tabs or bookmarks.
- No language learning distractions: Zero cognitive energy burned on foreign languages as a blocker. Execute with current profile and English medium of instruction.

## 3. Cognitive & Behavioral Traps

- No blaming other people: Zero victim mindset. Stop projecting delays, past educational gaps, or frustrations onto parents, college, or circumstances. External blame is a cowardly surrender of agency.
- Radical personal responsibility: I am 100% responsible for my actions, my discipline, my daily output, and my reality. No excuses, no rationalizations.
- No multitasking: Single-task focus only. One cognitive mode per block. If coding, only the editor and documentation open. If reading, only the book.
- No all-or-nothing spiraling: If a morning block or routine breaks, reset immediately on the next block. Never write off the whole day or wait for next Monday.
- No casual moderation on addictive traps: You do not moderate well. You are all-or-nothing. Zero mobile games, zero porn, zero infinite scrolling.
- No cluttered workspace: Keep the physical desk clean. Visual clutter creates cognitive friction.

## 4. Routine & Transition Traps

- Do not disturb the plans: Zero tinkering, redesigning, or second-guessing the roadmap once established. Execute the plan as written.
- Do not disturb the goals: The North Star is locked. Zero wandering eyes, zero shiny object syndrome, zero existential drift.
- Do not disturb routines and habits: Protect the daily structure unconditionally. Never negotiate with mood, low motivation, or temporary discomfort.
- No phone during meals: Breakfast, lunch, and dinner are screen-free anchors. Eating while watching videos triggers dopamine grazing and destroys the routine.
- No phone first thing in the morning: Wake up, drink water, brush, workout. Do not touch notifications or social media before the morning deep work block.
- No late-night screens: Turn off screens 30 to 60 minutes before bed. Blue light and late stimulation wreck sleep quality and ruin the next morning.

## 5. Financial Traps

- No consumer debt: Asset-backed debt only. Preserve optionality and cash runway.
- No lifestyle inflation: Maintain the minimal core budget (₹2,500 food, strict ₹7,100 fixed expenses) while accumulating liquid investment capital and building runway.
- No subscription bleeding: Audit and cancel recurring AI and dev subscriptions that are not actively producing output for today's milestone.

## 6. Health & Diet Traps

- No refined carbohydrates: Avoid maida, white bread, pastries, and processed flour that cause rapid blood sugar spikes and insulin surges.
- No ultra-processed and trans fats: Avoid fried snacks, commercial baked goods, hydrogenated oils, and fast food that elevate LDL cholesterol and cause brain fog.
- No liquid sugar: Avoid sugary sodas, commercial packaged fruit juices, and sweetened energy drinks.
- No skipping workouts: Physical grit and calisthenics are non-negotiable anchors. Slacking on training directly reduces mental stamina.
