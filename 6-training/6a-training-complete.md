# Complete Training Guide

Consolidated training notes for calisthenics and dumbbell workouts.

---

## Training Principles

### Warm-Up Protocol
Before every workout, warm up the most important joints:
- Shoulders
- Wrists
- Knees
- Hips

Example warm-up: Bodyweight pushups and squats (light, 10-15 reps each)

### Muscle Protein Synthesis (MPS)
- Beginners: Elevated for 2 days after training
- Advanced trainees: Elevated for 12 hours after training

Implications:
- For current level: More frequent muscle hits = more MPS spikes
- Distribute sets across training sessions
- Recovery happens in 1 day
- Hit muscles frequently for better results

### Training Frequency
- 5-6 training days per week
- 1-2 recovery days (light walking or mobility work, not complete rest)
- Progressive overload: Add 1-2 reps per set every 3-4 days
- Quality over quantity: Master proper form first

### Post-Workout Nutrition
- Have protein snack within 30 minutes of training
- Options: Soyabean seeds, curd bowl, or eggs
- Aids muscle repair and recovery

## Exercise Form Tips

### Key Principles
1. Control the weight - Don't let momentum do the work
2. Full range of motion - Complete each rep fully
3. Breathe properly - Exhale on exertion, inhale on release
4. Progressive overload - Gradually increase weight or reps
5. Rest periods - 60-90 seconds for hypertrophy, 2-3 minutes for strength

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
- Using too much weight (sacrificing form)
- Incomplete range of motion
- Rushing through exercises
- Not warming up properly
- Skipping rest days

## Recovery & Rest

### Rest Days
- Active recovery: Light walking, stretching, mobility work
- Complete rest: Only if feeling very fatigued or ill
- Sleep: 7-9 hours nightly for optimal recovery

### Recovery Indicators
- Good recovery: Feeling strong, motivated, no excessive soreness
- Poor recovery: Persistent fatigue, decreased performance, excessive soreness
- Adjust training: Reduce volume or intensity if recovery is poor

## Training Integration with Nutrition

### Pre-Workout
- Light meal 1-2 hours before training
- Hydrate well (water + electrolytes if needed)

### Post-Workout (Critical Window: 30 minutes)
- Protein: 20-30g within 30 minutes
- Options: Soyabean seeds, curd bowl, eggs, or protein shake
- Carbs: Small amount to replenish glycogen (optional)

### Daily Protein Distribution
- Spread protein intake throughout the day
- Aim for 150-170g protein/day (for 80kg bodyweight)
- 4-5 meals with protein each

## Progress Tracking

### Weekly Measurements
- Monday morning: Fasted weight
- Body measurements: Waist, chest, arms (optional)
- Progress photos: Front, back, side (same lighting)

### Training Log
Track for each workout:
- Exercise name
- Sets × Reps
- Weight used
- Notes (form, difficulty, etc.)

### Progressive Overload Strategy
- Week 1-2: Establish baseline
- Week 3-4: Add 1-2 reps per set
- Week 5-6: Increase weight by 2.5-5kg
- Week 7+: Continue progressive overload
