Biography
Yang Feng is an associate professor and Ph.D. supervisor at Nanjing University. His research focuses on two directions: software quality assurance and intelligent technologies, and language-based software engineering.

Software quality assurance and intelligent technologies
He develops testing and debugging techniques to improve the reliability and robustness of complex software and AI systems. Representative work includes DeepGini (ISSTA 2020) for prioritizing tests for deep neural networks, QATest (ASE 2022) for fuzzing question-answering systems, and RediI (ICSE 2025) for deterministic failure reproduction in distributed systems. His work also includes TACO (ICSE 2026) for assessing the trustworthiness of large language models in coding assistance, and AtPatch (ICSE 2026) for debugging Transformer models by correcting anomalous attention during inference. His recent research further investigates the behavioral trustworthiness and security of large language models and agents.

Language-based software engineering
He develops techniques and tools for improving the reliability of Rust software and compilers, and studies trustworthy C/C++-to-Rust translation. His work includes FRIES for fuzzing Rust library interactions, PALM for improving Rust unit test coverage, and RustGTC for constraint-guided API test generation. He also investigates code–comment inconsistency detection through RustC4/RustC4++, compiler fix-suggestion testing through SugBreaker (FSE 2026), and panic-bug analysis and repair through PanicFI. His research has received multiple Distinguished Paper Awards at ASE 2022, ESEC/FSE 2023, and Internetware 2023.

His work has also earned strong recognition from industry and open-source communities. His honors include Huawei’s Spark Award and Innovation Practice Award, the Xiaomi Youth Science and Technology Award, and the Xuanwu Community Outstanding Contribution Award. He was also recognized as a 2024 Annual Partner by Ant Group’s TuGraph community.
